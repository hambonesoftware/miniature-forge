This entire model, its materials and its renderer are generated locally. No 3D models, textures, libraries or fonts are downloaded.

Fly: W A S D moves through the scene; Q / E lowers and raises you. Overview supports drag and scroll. H shows performance statistics.

Outdoor access comes first. Danville's facade is photograph-inspired, with estimated dimensions and generic storefront names. Rear elevations are inferred.

Technical inspiration: Atlantis by Noniv. Independently implemented with WebGL2 for broader device support. Facade reference: Warren LeMay.
