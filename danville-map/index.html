Build the town on its real layout

Drag to pan the map; scroll or pinch to zoom. Switch to Aerial to compare the footprint positions with official orthophotography. The opacity slider fades footprint overlays. Click a footprint in Map or Aerial to inspect its height basis and coordinates.

3D layout generates actual polygonal building volumes and a sampled terrain mesh. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, Shift-drag to pan. Fly uses W A S D, Q / E for height and Shift for speed. The 3D aerial checkbox applies the same georeferenced image to the terrain.

This is a geographic massing preview. Facades, roof shapes, bridges and detailed street furniture are not reconstructed. Source height fields may themselves be estimates; orange buildings use a floor-count estimate or a six-metre fallback. Road ribbons have schematic widths. The 129 × 129 elevation grid samples the terrain at roughly 62 metres, with no vertical exaggeration.

The 8 × 8 km window is not the municipal boundary. The imagery is a saved reference from the 2022–2024 Phase 3 program, not a live satellite feed. Footprints and imagery may differ in capture date and roof/ground alignment.
